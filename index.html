02 / METHOD

From question to bounded finding.

The public record is not the entire experiment. It is a structured account of what was tested and what may responsibly be concluded.

01Bound the question

Specify the failure shape and where the claim applies.

02Fix the source

Name versions, commits and runtime assumptions.

03Run a controlled test

Compare conditions; record what actually happened.

04Inspect the evidence

Separate results, inference and unanswered questions.

05State both sides

What it can prove. What it cannot prove.
