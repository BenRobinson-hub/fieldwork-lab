When a negative result is useful

A test can be useful even without reproducing the reported failure. What changes is the evidence available for a specific configuration and window of observation—not the truth of every historical report.

BOUNDARY This is a methodological inference drawn from the published bounded follow-up, not a new observation of RocketMQ behavior.

Why the fixture matters

An experiment describes a relationship among a question, a system, its controlled conditions and an observation. When the fixture differs from the reported environment, the difference belongs in the result—not in fine print.
