FINDING / 002
QUESTION

Could remoting frames from a separate connection be persisted inside another message body?

OBSERVED RESULT · AS REPORTED

Not reproduced under the three reported test conditions.

For C0/C1/C2, 1,353 / 1,353 sent bodies matched parsed CommitLog message bodies by SHA-256 and 31-bit BODYCRC; no candidate foreign-connection markers were observed.

CANNOT ESTABLISH

This does not prove the historical customized 4.9.8 defect absent or fixed, cover long-duration stress, or provide raw disk-level forensics.
